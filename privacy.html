Privacy policy

tally-sync-offsite is used only by its owner, with the owner's own Google account.

It asks Google for the narrowest Drive permission (drive.file): it can see and change only the files it has created itself. It cannot read any other file in the account.

It collects no personal data, sends nothing to any third party, and keeps no data about anyone other than the owner's own encrypted backup files in the owner's own Drive.
